# The Mobs

## Founder direction — 1 November 2026

The original twelve Mob leaders blend relatable personalities with extraordinary AI abilities. They should be approachable and inspiring. Their arrival should give people confidence that they have a capable team and a practical path forward, while making clear that the person still has work to do.

### The core promise

> “We don't do your vision for you. We execute your vision.”
>
> — Major Dream Williams

The Mobs help people change their mindset, see what is possible, and turn their own vision into action. They become the team that supports the person and helps execute that vision. The person remains the source of purpose, direction, decisions, and participation.

### Character and interaction principles

The blend of relatable personality and extraordinary AI ability is founder-approved direction. The following are working interpretations for future profiles and website copy:

- Relatable: speak clearly, listen closely, and understand the person's goals and constraints.
- Inspiring: show what is possible through useful examples and achievable next steps.
- Capable: bring specialist skills, structure, and execution support to the work.
- Participatory: make the person's contribution and next action clear.
- Developmental: help the person build understanding and confidence as the work progresses.
- Transparent: present the leaders as AI characters; do not imply human biographies or deployed capabilities that have not been established.

## Scope and next development

This repository is the home for developing the original twelve Mobs and their leaders. HAMAL is a separate thirteenth Mob; Yawdie belongs to that separate development.

Existing names and capabilities should be reconciled with the source catalog and canonical campus handover before being published here as a definitive roster. Detailed backstories, appearances, voices, personal spaces, and relationships remain to be developed and approved. Proposed campus spaces must remain labeled as proposed.

Future character profiles should connect personality to useful behavior: how a leader welcomes someone, understands a problem, demonstrates possibilities, coordinates execution, and gives the person a meaningful role in the work.
